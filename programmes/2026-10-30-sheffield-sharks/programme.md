Seven from seven in the SLB, and straight back from a EuroCup win on Wednesday, the Lions host Sheffield Sharks at New City College Hackney tonight. The Sharks sit seventh on two wins from four. [src:gs-standings] [src:gs-opponent] [src:club-1]

London come into the night with momentum in both competitions: a 79–61 win over Türk Telekom Ankara in midweek and an 82–76 win over Bristol on Saturday. [src:gs-schedule-49597] [src:club-1] [src:club-2]

## Form: 7–0 in the SLB, 3–2 in Europe

Wednesday's EuroCup win was a game of two halves. The sides were level at 39–39 at the break, then the Lions outscored Türk Telekom 40–22 after it. Head coach Tautvydas Sabonis said: “At half-time, they understood the message. They levelled up their physicality and energy and executed the game plan to a tee.” [src:club-1]

Joel Scott was the star, with 21 points and 13 rebounds, and basketballsphere.com named him its MVP of the round. Tarik Phillip added 15 points and five assists. [src:club-1] [src:gn-3]

On Saturday the Lions led by 16 in the third quarter against Bristol before the Flyers closed to a three-point game, and London held on 82–76. Deane Williams led the scoring with 17. Sabonis wanted more: “We’ve got to understand that we need to go 40 minutes, possession after possession, and finish out games.” [src:club-2]
