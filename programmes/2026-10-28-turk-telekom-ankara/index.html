The Lions are back at the Copper Box tonight for a EuroCup meeting with Turk Telekom Ankara, three days after an 82–76 win over Bristol kept them unbeaten at 7–0 in the SLB.

In group B the Lions are fourth on 2–2, Ankara eighth on 1–3, and the Turkish side won both meetings last season.

Form 2–2 in Europe, 7–0 in the SLB

The season started with an 89–77 home win over La Laguna Tenerife, followed by an 81–76 defeat to Skyliners Frankfurt at the Copper Box. Since then the Lions have beaten Cheshire Phoenix 87–83 in the SLB, won 89–72 at U-BT Cluj-Napoca to level their EuroCup record at 2–2, and seen off Bristol Flyers 82–76.

At Cluj the second half decided it. London led 40–37 at the break, then took control in the third quarter, with Thomas Kennedy scoring 16 points on 7/7 from two-point range and Joel Scott adding 15 points and five rebounds. Head coach Tautvydas Sabonis said: “Our defence, 72 points is crazy.”

Against Bristol the Lions built a 16-point lead in the third quarter and had to hold on after a late comeback. Deane Williams led the scoring with 17, Maxwell Lewis added 15 and Ethan Price had 10 points, 5 rebounds and 3 assists.

Injury and availability

Keenan Evans is no longer with the Lions. Žalgiris Kaunas recalled him from his loan after an injury to their guard Nigel Williams-Goss. He played four games for London and averaged 11.8 points and 2.8 assists in the EuroCup.
