Eight from eight in the SLB, the Lions return from Rome with a point to prove and a winless Leicester side at New City College Hackney. The league leaders sit top of the table; the Riders are still looking for their first win of the campaign. [src:gs-standings] [src:opp-1]

Wednesday's EuroCup trip ended in an 86–84 overtime defeat, and the Lions are back at home in the league three days later. [src:club-1]

## Form: 8–0 in the SLB, a close one lost in Rome

The Lions came from 31–15 down after the first quarter to beat Sheffield 85–76 last Friday and move to 8–0. Maxwell Lewis III scored 20 with four threes from six, and Devante Jones added 12 points and eight assists. Head coach Tautvydas Sabonis said: “The second, third, and fourth quarter we played our basketball, the problem was our first quarter.” [src:club-2]

In Rome on Wednesday they led 44–42 at the break, fell 13 behind in the third and fought back to level at 76 when Lewis converted an and-one with 25.3 seconds left. Roma won it in overtime, 86–84, and the Lions finished 10-for-23 from the free-throw line. “We missed some open shots, and if I'm not mistaken, we missed 15 free throws, maybe even 20 free throws, in a game you lose in overtime,” said Sabonis. [src:club-1]
