Tonight at the Copper Box the Lions get the chance to answer September. Siauliai beat them 74–71 on their own floor in the opening weeks of the EuroCup, and the sides meet again with London second in Group B on 4–3 and the Lithuanians sixth on 3–4. Tip-off is at 7:30pm. [src:el-games] [src:el-standings] [src:club-1]

It is a busy week to be a Lions supporter. The club top the SLB Championship at 9–1 after Friday's comeback against Surrey, signed a 19-year-old guard on loan from Zalgiris Kaunas on Sunday, and brought Ade Adebayo back on a short-term contract. Siauliai arrive having beaten Gargzdai at home in their own league on Saturday, three days after a heavy EuroCup defeat to Tenerife. [src:club-2] [src:club-1] [src:club-3] [src:opp-1] [src:el-games]

## Form: late comebacks at the Copper Box

The Lions have won two of their last three EuroCup and SLB home games by coming from behind, and lost the other heavily. Venice led 69–59 with 6:41 left on Tuesday of last week after opening the fourth quarter with 14 unanswered points. London answered with a 12–0 run, finished by Thomas Kennedy's offensive rebound and basket, Landrius Horton's three with 33 seconds left and two Joel Scott free throws, to win 80–76. [src:club-4] [src:el-games]

“It's a very big win when you have a lot of adversity, a lot of injuries, and guys still believe, and guys execute,” said head coach Tautvydas Sabonis. Kennedy had 16 points, eight rebounds and three assists. [src:club-4]

Friday brought a similar story in the SLB. The Lions led 48–42 at half-time, were behind 64–59 after Surrey outscored them 22–11 in the third quarter, and won 80–77 when Horton finished an and-one with 10.3 seconds left. Horton had 16 points and seven assists. Sabonis said the Lions “forgot how to play our brand of basketball” in the middle quarters before putting it together in the last. [src:club-2] [src:gs-schedule-49597]

The other results in the run were mixed: an 85–76 win over Sheffield on 30 October, an 84–86 defeat at Maxima Roma on 4 November, and a 76–97 home defeat to Leicester on 6 November. [src:gs-schedule-49597] [src:el-games]

## Lions roster: and who to watch

### Joel Scott

Leads the Lions in EuroCup scoring on 14.6 points a game and in rebounding on 6.3. Scored 14 points against Venice and 15 against Surrey, and made the two free throws that completed the 12–0 run on Tuesday. [src:el-stats] [src:club-4] [src:club-2]

### Thomas Kennedy

The centre averages 12.3 points and five rebounds in 22 minutes. Led London with 16 points and eight rebounds against Venice, converting a steal and an offensive rebound into baskets in the comeback, and added 10 points and seven rebounds against Surrey. [src:el-stats] [src:club-4] [src:club-2]

### Landrius Horton

Averages 7.7 points, three rebounds and two assists, but is the man the Lions have turned to late. Hit three threes in his 15 points against Venice, including the one with 33 seconds left, and had the decisive and-one against Surrey. [src:el-stats] [src:club-4] [src:club-2]

### Maxwell Lewis III

Averages 5.9 points in 13 minutes, but scored 14 against Venice and 14 against Surrey, with four rebounds and three assists in the latter. Knocked down a three and a transition windmill dunk among his baskets. [src:el-stats] [src:club-4] [src:club-2]

### Tarik Phillip

The guard leads the Lions in assists on 3.9 a game and in steals on 1.4, while averaging nine points in 22 minutes. [src:el-stats]

## Injury and availability

The club described the Lions as playing with a limited roster against Venice, citing “a lot of injuries”, and named nobody. Ade Adebayo, a London-born guard who averaged 14.4 points in Spain last season, rejoined on a short-term contract on Friday, and Grunkis has just arrived. [src:club-4] [src:club-3] [src:club-1]

For Siauliai, Cedric Henderson Jr returned against Tenerife last Wednesday after several weeks out injured, scoring 12 points with four rebounds in 17 minutes. Tauras Jogela had still not played, and head coach Darius Songaila said before that game that he did not expect Jogela to help. Michael Caffey and Jogela both missed Saturday's league win over Gargzdai. [src:opp-2] [src:opp-4] [src:opp-1]

## The opposition: Siauliai, 3–4

Siauliai have won three of seven in Group B. They beat Maxima Roma 98–93 at home and Turk Telekom Ankara 94–84 away, but lost 75–94 to Cluj in their opener, 77–98 at Venice and 71–86 at home to Tenerife. [src:el-games] [src:el-standings]

Against Tenerife, the Lithuanians scored 12 points in the second quarter and went into half-time 38–27 down. Martynas Pacevicius made all four of his two-point attempts for 11, and Dayvion Mcknight and Dovydas Romancenko each scored 10. [src:opp-2]

Songaila described his team's form as up and down, saying he wanted more consistency and that travel and the Lithuanian championship were not helping. On Saturday they beat Gargzdai 97–82 to move to 5–5 in the league, with Simas Jarumbauskas scoring 24. [src:opp-4] [src:opp-1]

### Cedric Henderson Jr

The Siauliai scorer averages 15.5 points, taking six threes a game and making 2.5 of them. Back from injury last week, he played 17 minutes against Tenerife. [src:el-stats] [src:opp-2]

### Simas Jarumbauskas

Averages 9.7 points in 18 minutes off the bench. Scored 24 points on Saturday against Gargzdai, with three threes from six attempts and the free throws that sealed the win, in what the club called a career game in the Lithuanian league. [src:el-stats] [src:opp-1]

### Michael Caffey

The guard averages 4.3 assists in 16 minutes. Before the Tenerife game the club's website noted his 15-assist night in a EuroCup defeat, which came one short of the competition record, and he said passing is his passion. He sat out Saturday's league game. [src:el-stats] [src:opp-3] [src:opp-1]

### Dayvion Mcknight

Leads Siauliai in assists on 4.6 and steals on 1.7 a game. Had 13 points, six rebounds and six assists against Gargzdai. [src:el-stats] [src:opp-1]

Dominykas Grunkis joined on loan from EuroLeague side Zalgiris Kaunas until the end of the season. General manager Martynas Purlys said the club “see great potential in him”. Adebayo, who started 16 games for Sol Girones Bisbal Basket last season, is back on a short-term contract after an earlier spell with the Lions this season. [src:club-1] [src:club-3]
