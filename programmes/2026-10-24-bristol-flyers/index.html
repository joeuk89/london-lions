Six wins from six in the SLB Championship and fresh off a win in Romania, the Lions welcome the side that sits second, Bristol Flyers, to the Copper Box Arena. Bristol are 4–2, and they were the closest to London in September, when the Lions won 80–78 at the SGS College Arena.

It is a week of change in the Lions backcourt, with Keenan Evans recalled to Žalgiris Kaunas, and a Bristol side led by Davion Bailey's 18.3 points a game. Here is what to know before tip-off.

Form 6–0 in the SLB, 2–2 in the EuroCup

The Lions beat Cheshire Phoenix 87–83 on the road on Sunday 18 October. After a back-and-forth first half they closed it with a 10–0 run to lead 45–35 at the break, then had to withstand a series of Cheshire runs after the interval. Landrius Horton led the scoring with 17 points, shooting 4/5 from three, with Tarik Phillip and Thomas Kennedy adding 14 each. After Cheshire made it a one-point game with 13 seconds left, Joel Scott was fouled and made both free throws.

Three days later they won 89–72 at U-BT Cluj-Napoca to move to 2–2 in the EuroCup. London trailed 22–21 after the first quarter and led 40–37 at half-time before taking control after the interval, using defensive pressure and efficient scoring. Head coach Tautvydas Sabonis pointed to the defence: “Our defence, 72 points is crazy,” said Sabonis.

The EuroCup has not been all smooth. The Lions beat La Laguna Tenerife 89–77 at home on 6 October, then lost 81–76 to Skyliners Frankfurt at home on 15 October. In the league, they edged Leicester Riders 71–66 on 2 October.

Injury and availability

Keenan Evans will not be in the Lions squad. The club confirmed on Friday that he is returning to Žalgiris Kaunas following his loan, after being recalled because of an injury to Žalgiris guard Nigel Williams-Goss. Evans made four appearances in both EuroCup and SLB Championship play, averaging 9.8 points and 3.5 assists in the SLB Championship. He scored 12 points at Cluj on Wednesday.

General Manager Martynas Purlys said: “Having Keenan Evans with us has been a great opportunity. He brought quality, experience and leadership to our backcourt, and we wish him all the best as he returns to Žalgiris Kaunas.”

Bristol Flyers second, 4–2

Bristol have won four of six in the league. Their losses are to the Lions and, on 25 September, 94–68 at Newcastle Eagles. Since then they beat Manchester Basketball 76–58 at home, won 78–73 at Leicester Riders and then edged Liverpool Basketball 90–89 on Sunday. That one was settled at the line: Davion Bailey's lefty lay-in put the Flyers one up with 16 seconds left, and Liverpool's Damiri Lindo missed two free throws with just over a second to play.

Bristol's recap singled out the pairing of Matteus Case and Kasen Jennings in the backcourt as dynamic, and the forward combination of Mike Diggins Jr and Zach Anderson for keeping them in touch in the first half. Head coach Andreas Kapoulas and Cam Holden were on the club podcast this week looking back on the one-point win.
