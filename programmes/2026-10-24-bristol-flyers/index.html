Six wins from six in the SLB Championship, and back from Romania with a EuroCup win, the Lions host Bristol Flyers at the Copper Box Arena on Saturday. Bristol sit second on 4–2, the only side within a win of the Lions at the top.

The sides have met once already this season: the Lions won 80–78 at the SGS College Arena on 18 September.

Form 6–0 in the SLB, 2–2 in Europe

The week began with a close one at Cheshire on Sunday. The Lions led 45–35 at the break after a 10–0 run to close the half, with Tarik Phillip at the heart of it, then had to withstand a series of Cheshire runs before winning 87–83. Joel Scott's free throws with 13 seconds left stretched the lead, and Thomas Kennedy sealed it from the line with two seconds to go.

Landrius Horton led the scoring with 17, shooting 4 of 5 from three, with Phillip and Kennedy on 14 each. Phillip added seven assists, and Scott had 13 points and some crucial late baskets.

Wednesday in Romania was a different kind of win. After a first half that ended 40–37 to the Lions, the second half belonged to London: a defence that made life increasingly difficult for Cluj, a lead that reached 17, and a final score of 89–72. Kennedy had 16 points and shot 7 of 7 from two-point range, Scott added 15 points and five rebounds, and Devante Jones finished with 14 points, five rebounds, four assists and three steals. Head coach Tautvydas Sabonis pointed to the defence: "Our defence, 72 points is crazy."

That result followed a 76–81 home defeat to Skyliners Frankfurt in the EuroCup on 15 October, and the win in Cluj leaves the Lions 2–2 in Europe. Before that came an 89–77 win over La Laguna Tenerife and a 71–66 league win over Leicester Riders.

Bristol Flyers second, 4–2

Andreas Kapoulas's Flyers have won four of their last five, with the one defeat a 68–94 loss at Newcastle Eagles on 25 September. Last Sunday they beat Liverpool Basketball 90–89 at home, with Davion Bailey's lefty lay-in giving them a one-point lead with 16 seconds left; Liverpool's Damiri Lindo then missed two free throws with just over a second on the clock.

Before that they won 78–73 at Leicester Riders and 76–58 at home to Manchester Basketball, and they opened with a 101–92 win at Cheshire Phoenix. The club has also been in Germany this week for its ENBL European campaign.
