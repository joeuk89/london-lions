Five wins from five across the SLB and EuroCup, and now a trip to Italy. The Lions are second in EuroCup Group B on 3–2 and visit Maxima Roma, who sit sixth on 2–3 and come off a five-point defeat in Lithuania. [src:el-standings] [src:el-games]

A win in Rome would keep the Lions close to Group B's leaders, La Laguna Tenerife. [src:el-standings]

## Form: five wins on the bounce

The Lions' week was Wednesday and Friday at home. Against Türk Telekom Ankara in the EuroCup it was level at 39–39 at the break, then London outscored the visitors 40–22 in the second half to win 79–61. "At half-time, they understood the message," said head coach Tautvydas Sabonis. "They levelled up their physicality and energy and executed the game plan to a tee." [src:club-2] [src:el-games]

Two days later it went the other way. Sheffield led 31–15 after the first quarter at New City College Hackney, yet the Lions came back to win 85–76 and move to 8–0 in the SLB. Sabonis said the problem was the opening quarter: "The second, third, and fourth quarter we played our basketball, the problem was our first quarter." [src:club-1] [src:gs-schedule-49597]

Before that came a 82–76 win over Bristol and an 89–72 win at U-BT Cluj-Napoca, with an 87–83 win at Cheshire Phoenix before it. [src:el-games] [src:gs-schedule-49597]

## The opposition: sixth, 2–3

Roma have a win over Reyer Venice, 90–79, and a heavy one over Cluj, 89–55, but lost at Tenerife 96–71 and at Ankara 92–77. Last Wednesday Siauliai won 98–93, with Siauliai hitting 17 three-pointers according to one preview. [src:el-games] [src:gn-6]

Matteo Cotelli's team opened their Serie A season with two defeats and have since won three in a row, including 81–75 against Udine on Sunday. The same preview put them second in three-point percentage in the league after four rounds at 40.4%, but among the weakest rebounding sides. [src:gn-6]
