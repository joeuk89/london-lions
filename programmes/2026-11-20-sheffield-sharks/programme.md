The Lions go to Sheffield top of the SLB Championship on 9–1, three days after a 68–55 EuroCup win over Šiauliai. Sheffield sit sixth on 4–4, and lost 85–76 to London in Hackney at the end of October. [src:gs-standings] [src:club-1] [src:gs-opponent]

It is a Friday night at the Canon Medical Arena, tip-off 7.30 pm, with a trip to Manchester to follow on Sunday. [src:gs-schedule-49597]

## Form: 9–1 in the SLB, 5–3 in Europe

The only league defeat came against Leicester, 97–76 at home on 6 November. Since then the Lions have won three in a row across both competitions: 80–76 over Reyer Venice, 80–77 over Surrey and 68–55 over Šiauliai. Before Leicester they lost 86–84 at Maxima Roma. [src:gs-schedule-49597] [src:el-games]

The Surrey game was the closest of them. The Lions trailed 64–59 going into the fourth quarter, and Landrius Horton finished through contact for the and-one with 10.3 seconds left to make it 80–77. Head coach Tautvydas Sabonis said the team had “forgot how to play our brand of basketball” in the middle quarters, “but then, in the last quarter, when it mattered most, we finally put it together.” [src:club-4]

Against Šiauliai on Tuesday the defence did the work: the visitors scored 19 points across the last two quarters. Maxwell Lewis III had 24 points, five steals and three assists. “Those are kind of the ugly, nasty games that I love,” said Sabonis. [src:club-1]
