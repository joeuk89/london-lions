The Lions are top of the SLB Championship on 8–1 and welcome Surrey 89ers, who sit ninth on 3–4, to the Copper Box Arena.

London arrive off a Tuesday night in which they came from ten down to beat Umana Reyer Venice in the EuroCup. Surrey come off a 68–53 win at Bristol on Sunday, and last season's five meetings between the sides are listed under Head to head.

Form 8–1 in the SLB

Leicester Riders beat the Lions 97–76 at NCC Hackney on 6 November after leading 41–39 at half-time. Thomas Kennedy scored 20 points on 8-12 shooting, but Leicester shot 50% from the field and scored 16 second-chance points, according to the club's report.

On Tuesday the Lions beat Umana Reyer Venice 80–76 at the Copper Box in the EuroCup. They trailed 69–59 with 6:41 remaining after Venice opened the fourth quarter with 14 unanswered points, then went on a 12–0 run: Kennedy scored inside, Maxwell Lewis III hit a three, and Landrius Horton's three with 33 seconds left made it 78–74. Kennedy finished with 16 points, eight rebounds and three assists; Horton had 15, and Lewis III and Joel Scott 14 each.

"It's a very big win when you have a lot of adversity, a lot of injuries, and guys still believe, and guys execute," said head coach Tautvydas Sabonis, whose side played with what the club called a limited roster.

Before those two came an 84–86 EuroCup defeat at Maxima Roma on 4 November and an 85–76 home win over Sheffield Sharks on 30 October.

Surrey 89ers ninth, 3–4

Surrey won 68–53 at Bristol on Sunday, turning the game around in the third quarter. The scores were level at 37–37 early in the period when they went on a 13–1 run, and Bristol managed only four points in the quarter. Head coach Lloyd Gardner said: "The third quarter won us the game. We were behind at half-time, but we defended properly after the break and held them to four points, and that gave us some freedom at the other end."

Before that they lost 81–76 to Sheffield Sharks, beat Cheshire Phoenix 107–91 away, lost 82–55 at Newcastle Eagles, and beat Leicester Riders 107–102.

Club news

The Lions told City AM they remain focused on becoming London's first EuroLeague team from 2030, though the paper reports they now sit behind four more teams in the pecking order. "Building an elite performance structure, developing our facilities and a gold standard of basketball in the UK remain central to our long-term plans," said president Lenz Balan. City AM also notes the club's new kit deal with Nike's Jordan brand.
