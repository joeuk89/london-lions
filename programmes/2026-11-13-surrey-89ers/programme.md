The Lions are top of the SLB Championship on 8–1 and welcome Surrey 89ers, who sit ninth on 3–4, to the Copper Box Arena. [src:gs-standings] [src:gs-lions-stats] [src:gs-opponent]

London arrive off a Tuesday night in which they came from ten down to beat Umana Reyer Venice in the EuroCup. Surrey come off a 68–53 win at Bristol on Sunday, and last season's five meetings between the sides are listed under Head to head. [src:club-1] [src:opp-1] [src:gs-41897-178238-schedule]

## Form: 8–1 in the SLB

Leicester Riders beat the Lions 97–76 at NCC Hackney on 6 November after leading 41–39 at half-time. Thomas Kennedy scored 20 points on 8-12 shooting, but Leicester shot 50% from the field and scored 16 second-chance points, according to the club's report. [src:club-2] [src:nr-1]

On Tuesday the Lions beat Umana Reyer Venice 80–76 at the Copper Box in the EuroCup. They trailed 69–59 with 6:41 remaining after Venice opened the fourth quarter with 14 unanswered points, then went on a 12–0 run: Kennedy scored inside, Maxwell Lewis III hit a three, and Landrius Horton's three with 33 seconds left made it 78–74. Kennedy finished with 16 points, eight rebounds and three assists; Horton had 15, and Lewis III and Joel Scott 14 each. [src:club-1] [src:gs-schedule-49597] [src:el-games]

"It's a very big win when you have a lot of adversity, a lot of injuries, and guys still believe, and guys execute," said head coach Tautvydas Sabonis, whose side played with what the club called a limited roster. [src:club-1]

Before those two came an 84–86 EuroCup defeat at Maxima Roma on 4 November and an 85–76 home win over Sheffield Sharks on 30 October. [src:el-games] [src:gs-schedule-49597]

## The opposition: ninth, 3–4

Surrey won 68–53 at Bristol on Sunday, turning the game around in the third quarter. The scores were level at 37–37 early in the period when they went on a 13–1 run, and Bristol managed only four points in the quarter. Head coach Lloyd Gardner said: "The third quarter won us the game. We were behind at half-time, but we defended properly after the break and held them to four points, and that gave us some freedom at the other end." [src:opp-1]

Before that they lost 81–76 to Sheffield Sharks, beat Cheshire Phoenix 107–91 away, lost 82–55 at Newcastle Eagles, and beat Leicester Riders 107–102. [src:gs-opponent]
