The league leaders travel to Manchester on Sunday looking for an answer to Friday's 98–83 defeat at Sheffield. The Lions are 9–2 and top of the SLB Championship; Manchester are seventh on 5–6. [src:gs-standings] [src:club-1] [src:opp-1]

It is a four o'clock tip at the National Basketball Performance Centre, the Lions' first visit to the city this season, on a double-header Sunday with the Manchester women's team playing at 1pm. [src:opp-1] [src:gs-schedule-49597]

## Form: a win and a defeat

The week began with a 68–55 EuroCup win over Šiauliai at the Copper Box, built on defence after the break: the Lions held Šiauliai to 19 points across the final two quarters. "In the second half, to leave a team at 55 in general, and 19, it's ridiculous," said head coach Tautvydas Sabonis. Maxwell Lewis III scored 24 with five steals, and Landrius Horton added 18. [src:club-2]

Friday was harder. Sheffield opened with a 12–2 run and led 24–13 after one quarter, 49–38 at half-time and 71–59 going into the last. Jaden Shackelford led the Lions with 17 points in his latest appearance for the club, Joel Scott had 14, Lewis 13 and Dominykas Grunkis 12, while Josh O'Garro and Thomas Kennedy each reached 11. [src:club-1]

Before that, the Lions beat Surrey 80–77 and Reyer Venice 80–76, and lost 97–76 at home to Leicester on 6 November. [src:gs-schedule-49597] [src:el-games]

## The opposition: seventh, 5–6

Manchester's last five league games read as a mixed run. They won at Leicester 89–87, at Bristol 78–74 and at Liverpool 91–85, then lost at home to Cheshire 93–90 and to Bristol 86–84 last time out at the NBPC. [src:gs-opponent]

Since then they have been to Kosovo and won 95–84 at BC Bashkimi in the FIBA Europe Cup on Wednesday, leading by 17 after three quarters, then holding off a late 10–0 run that cut an 18-point deficit to eight. Bristol were level at 72–72 in the fourth quarter before edging ahead last weekend. [src:opp-2] [src:opp-4]

## Head to head: three wins last season

The two sides met five times last season. Manchester won the first at Copper Box on 19 December, 91–74, and again 72–71 there on 4 March. The Lions won the other three: 82–74 at the NBPC on 22 February, 83–74 in the SLB Cup at the AO Arena on 22 March, and 91–86 at the NBPC on 19 April. ## Club news

Two guards have arrived in a week. The club announced Shackelford, a 6'3" American, on 17 November, and General Manager Martynas Purlys said "he brings valuable experience and a strong basketball background to our team". Grunkis, 19, was the club's other signing, on loan until the end of the season. [src:club-3] [src:club-4]

Off the court, club president Lenz Balan told Eurohoops the Lions applied to join the EuroLeague in 2030, citing the lack of their own arena as "the biggest sticking point". City AM reports EuroLeague CEO Chus Bueno has left the door open but says it may take longer. [src:gn-3] [src:gn-2]
