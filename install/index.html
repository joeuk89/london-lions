Install the Lions app

Every programme of the season, readable offline. Open the season

Install it once and every programme you open stays on your phone, even with no signal. Programmes only stay saved in the installed app, not in a browser tab.

iPhone

If you opened this from the Gmail app, open it in Safari first: tap the compass or the three dots, then “Open in Safari”. Gmail’s own browser can’t install apps.

  1. In Safari, tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add.
  4. Open Lions from your home screen.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu (three dots, top right).
  3. Tap Install app (on some phones, Add to Home screen), then Install.
  4. Open Lions from your home screen or app drawer.

After that

On game morning you get an email. Open the app once that day and the programme is saved for the rest of the season.
